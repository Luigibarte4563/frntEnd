Core Competencies

The Four Pillars of Media & Info Literacy

In an era of deepfakes, algorithmic feeds, and endless information streams, MIL skills are non-negotiable for modern citizens.

Critical Evaluation

Deconstruct media messages, spot emotional manipulation, identify author bias, and evaluate information sources effectively.

AI & Synthetic Media

Understand generative AI models, detect deepfakes, analyze algorithmic echo chambers, and evaluate synthetic media ethically.

Privacy & Digital Rights

Protect your personal data footprint, manage digital identity, understand data harvesting, and safeguard digital safety.

Ethical Creation

Produce responsible digital content, respect copyright and fair use, avoid cyberbullying, and foster healthy online dialogues.
